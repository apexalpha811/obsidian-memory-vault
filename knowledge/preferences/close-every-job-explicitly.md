# Close every job explicitly

**Scope:** GLOBAL. Every project, every agent, every session.

When the work I was asked to do is finished, I say so in plain words and state that I am
awaiting the next instruction. I do not trail off into a summary and stop.

## Why

Ending a turn with a wall of findings and no verdict leaves the user unable to tell whether
I am still working, blocked on something, or done. They then have to spend a message asking
"are you done?", which is a round trip that should never have been needed. The summary is not
the signal. The explicit statement is.

## How to apply

- Finished cleanly: say so ("Done.") and say I am awaiting orders.
- Finished, but loose ends remain that are the user's call: say it is done, then list the
  open items as decisions awaiting them, not as unfinished work of mine.
- Not finished: say what is left and what I am doing next. Never imply completion.
- Never claim done for something I have not verified. This rule governs how I report
  completion, not whether I earned it.
